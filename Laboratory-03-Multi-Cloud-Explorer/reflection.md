# Reflection

## My Multi-Cloud Experience

Exploring AWS, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud helped me understand that cloud platforms provide many similar services, but they have different strengths and approaches. Before this activity, I mostly knew cloud computing as a way of accessing resources over the internet. After researching the three platforms, I gained a better understanding of how organizations can choose cloud services based on their technical and business requirements.

The cloud platform that impressed me the most was **AWS**. I chose it because it offers a broad range of cloud services and gives me opportunities to learn different types of cloud technologies. I found its services and features useful for the type of workloads I am interested in learning about.

AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud have several similarities. All three provide virtual machines, object storage, databases, networking, identity management, and managed services. They also provide global infrastructure that allows organizations to deploy applications and services in different geographic locations.

Business requirements have an important influence on cloud provider selection. A startup may prioritize cost, scalability, and ease of development, while a university using Microsoft technologies may benefit more from Azure. An AI research organization may prefer a platform with strong AI and machine learning capabilities, while a global e-commerce company may prioritize availability, scalability, and worldwide infrastructure.

Acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant helped me understand that choosing a cloud provider is not simply about selecting the most popular platform. The decision should be based on the organization's goals, existing technology, budget, workload, security requirements, and future growth.

This activity also improved my GitHub portfolio skills. I practiced organizing technical documentation in Markdown, maintaining a structured repository, adding evidence through screenshots, and using Git to commit and push changes. Overall, the activity gave me a better understanding of how cloud technologies can be evaluated and matched to real-world business needs.
